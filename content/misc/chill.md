Once a day I do this with my dogs in some fashion or another. In this video, I'm listening to an audio book in my shed. Sometimes I'll do this in the house, with the family while watching a movie. Maybe I have company over, and we are hanging out by a fire and my dogs are acting a fool, so I'll put them in a down and work this chill training. In this video I'm shaping focus as well. That's not necessary; however, if you are doing chill training with just you and your pups, then you might as well practice.

To work on focus here, I mark eye contact before rewarding. I'm asking for about 10 seconds of sustained eye contact here, but it started with just an instant. Another thing to note is if you had sound in this video, you wouldn't hear a verbal marker at all. Instead, I mark the eye contact with a raising of my eyebrows. It might be silly but it's better than a verbal marker because if you use a verbal marker here, you might miss the eye-contact and mark your dog adverting their eyes. With an eyebrow raise, if you mark too late, it brings their eyes back to your face.
