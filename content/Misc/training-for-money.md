# My Philosophy
1. Teach with Food
2. Build drive with play/toys
3. Force perfection and/rules with pressure
- Competitive sports, the dog needs more pressure because we expect them to work till the end with little praise, stay in position, and perfection
- Pet dogs and non competitive sports can get away with more play and less pressure, depending on handler preferences
- Pet handlers who know nothing should progress from food, to toys, to slip leads, to prong collar, to e-collar in that order. Over punishment breaks dogs, normal people can't be trusted till they prove they are ready.

# Facility
To get started, we almost definattley need to build our customer base. To me that means board and training. I figure to make 100k a year we need to average at 2 dogs per person for 50 weeks out of the year. This means the facility should be able to house atleast 4 dogs, preferrably 6-8. We would need to have referalls ready for turning people down. If we worked military deployments then we have more guarenteed income, but less space for more dogs and we would have to charge less.

Best place to build this would be my property. Thinking like a 30x30 with fences, dog doors to outside areas, electricity, water, easy to clean surface probably concrete. Kennels would include inside and outside area. Then another spot for play/socialize with dogs. We'd want a freezer for raw fed dogs, storage of all the gear/food, running water, an office. 

# Guarentees
I would want to have guarentees for a given type of board and train. This means some sort of test depending on what we offered. 
For someone dropping a dog off for a week, it might be we guarentee a dog can do x, y, and z with luring.
For someone dropping a dog off for 4 weeks, it might be something more like dog can complete canine good citizen test with x number of corrections or less. 
For custom drop offs it might just be dog no longer does this, or dog is prepped for that. 
4 week drop offs should also prep people for club meet ups.

# Board and Trains
A board and train program would be customized for the individual with a guarentee. If we didn't accomplish the guarentee then we would offer to extend the time for free or refund a portion of the money if it turns out to be too much. 

In general I would suggest a program that's something like.
## Week 1
- Get to know the pup through play and charging a marker
- Teach with food lots of food, play if toy drive exists
### Private
- show how to get the dog to do things with the food, send home for atleast 1 day. Let customer come back with questions and requests
## Week 2
- Adjust based on customer feedback
- Build drive with toys if possible
- Fade lures
- Work more shaping
- Start Leash pressure
### Private
- Teach customer, have them come back again
## Week 3
- Enforce commands with leash pressure
- Start e-collar work if applicable
### Private
- Send home
## Week 4
- Generalize behaviors with different handlers in different locations. So you and me swap at this point and take dogs to parks, lowes, whatevers
- Prepare for test to demonstrate all guarentees are met. 

## For military long term board and trains
- Atleast 1 hour training a day, the rest can be play 
- Send links of progress and training every week

## Included
Board and trains of 4 weeks and longer should include:
- swag
- any gear we use in the training, so food pouche, leash, prong, e-collar
- videos
- unlimited refreshers, **scheduled of course**
